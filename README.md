Forecasts how many people will be watching Grand Theft Auto VI on Twitch in each hour of its launch day, as five quantiles per hour. It reads the launch's first hour and the release-day curves of 37 past big launches, and weights each past launch by how close its first-hour audience was to this launch's. Backtested leave-one-out on those 37 launches, its mean pinball loss on the log scale is 0.20, against 0.46 for the flat baseline.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_INPUT_DIR=input MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
